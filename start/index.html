WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

One place to move the bid forward.

  1. 1
    Get your matching shortlistAfter signup processing, look for your welcome email. Alerts normally arrive around 6 AM your time from the next morning, with a short update on quiet days.
  2. 2
    Open the bid in BidBellCreate and verify your workspace account with the same email. Open an opportunity from your alert and keep that bid and its response together.
  3. 3
    Reuse what your company knowsAdd the buyer's documents, reuse saved company facts and original files, and draft answers with source-linked AI. Complete supported fillable PDF text fields from reviewed facts, then check missing details and pricing. Answer drafts autosave privately; review and choose Save answer to add them to your response.
  4. 4
    Review and send your bidCheck the buyer’s submission recipients, message, price and files, then confirm a supported email send in BidBell. Portal bids continue through the buyer’s portal.
