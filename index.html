BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Clear expectations.
Better preparation.

How does one trial cover both?+

Start with your alert preferences, then create and verify your workspace account using the same email. Once your signup is processed, your existing alert trial dates also control preparation access. Registration does not restart or extend the trial. At the end, choose whether to continue for US$39/month.

What can I do in the workspace?+

Open the opportunity from your alert, add the buyer's documents, reuse saved company details and files from your company library, and build answers with source-linked AI. Complete supported fillable PDF text fields from explicitly reviewed facts. Check missing information, your buyer-facing price and exact files before sending a supported email bid or continuing in the buyer's portal. Review every AI suggestion.

Which businesses can use BidBell?+

Daily alerts currently support janitorial, carpet, window and exterior cleaning. The document preparation tools can help with other service bids you bring, but broader alert coverage is not included. Always check the full buyer notice and any specialist requirements.

Can I send my bid from BidBell?+

Yes, for supported email submissions where the original buyer instructions specify a single submission recipient. Review the current notice, exact deadline, message, price and attachments, then confirm the final send yourself. Portal-only bids use a guided handoff to the buyer's portal. BidBell does not sign declarations or automatically apply everywhere. Mail-server acceptance does not prove buyer receipt, and uncertain delivery is not automatically retried.

What happens to my pricing calculations?+

Your internal cost breakdown stays in your workspace. It is excluded from buyer-facing files. Only a price you explicitly choose and review belongs in your offer; review the exact message and attachments before sending.

What if a document is scanned or incomplete?+

Pages with no readable text need your attention. Check the original source, obtain a readable copy or arrange OCR separately. You remain responsible for reviewing all forms, attachments, deadlines and amendments.

Does a ready package guarantee a win?+

No. Readiness checks help identify missing answers and unfinished text. They do not certify compliance or eligibility, and the buyer decides which bid wins.
