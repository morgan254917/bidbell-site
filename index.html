BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Clear expectations.
Better preparation.

What can I do in the workspace?+

Create a project, upload PDF, DOCX or TXT documents, organize source-linked requirements, save company facts and responses, calculate private pricing and review a package before export. AI can suggest exact source requirements and relevant excerpts from your saved company facts. Review every suggestion before accepting it.

Is BidBell only for cleaning businesses?+

No. The uploaded-document preparation workflow is broader than cleaning and can support other service businesses. The current opportunity list is a selected snapshot, not complete industry coverage. Specialist or regulated bids still need the relevant expertise.

Will BidBell submit the bid for me?+

No. You check your response, confirm all commitments and submit through the buyer's required process. BidBell does not sign certifications or submit applications on your behalf.

What happens to my pricing calculations?+

Your internal cost breakdown stays in your workspace and is excluded from the exported response package. You choose the price and wording to include in your final response.

What if a document is scanned or incomplete?+

Pages with no readable text need your attention. Check the original source, obtain a readable copy or arrange OCR separately. You remain responsible for reviewing all forms, attachments, deadlines and amendments.

Does a ready package guarantee a win?+

No. Readiness checks help identify missing answers and unfinished text. They do not certify compliance or eligibility, and the buyer decides which bid wins.
