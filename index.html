Questions

Where do the bids come from?

From SAM.gov, the official US government website where federal agencies publish contract opportunities. Every bid in your email links to its official notice so you can check it yourself.

Are you part of the government?

No. BidBell is a private alert service and is not affiliated with SAM.gov, GSA or any government agency. SAM.gov registration is free; we never charge for it.

Do I need to be registered in SAM.gov?

To bid on federal contracts, yes: a free registration at sam.gov. You can receive BidBell alerts while your registration is in progress.

How many bids will I get?

It depends on your trade and states. Some days there are none, and then we send nothing. We do not pad the email with bids you cannot use.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, from the link in your payment receipt or by replying to any BidBell email. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
