Ask us a question ↗
Is this a bidding service or an alert service?+

BidBell combines a public opportunity workspace, a manual bid checklist and paid daily alerts for currently supported trades. We do not submit bids, automatically extract every requirement, guarantee eligibility or promise contract wins.

Which trades are available today?+

Daily alerts currently cover janitorial, carpet cleaning and building cleaning, using federal notices and selected state boards. Grounds maintenance and HVAC maintenance coverage are being validated and are not included in the live alert service yet. Sources and availability vary; this is not every public or private opportunity.

What happens if there are no matches?+

You still receive a short daily status email. New matches are not guaranteed. Reply to discuss adjusting your trades or states if the results are too narrow.

How do the trial and payment work?+

Start with 14 free days without a payment card. Near the end of the trial, we email you plan options. Continue for US$39 monthly or US$390 yearly. Paid subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Payments are handled by Paddle.

Can I change my alerts or cancel?+

Reply to a BidBell email to update your preferences or request cancellation. You can also manage a paid subscription through the link in your Paddle receipt. See our refund policy and terms.
