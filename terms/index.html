1. What BidBell is

BidBell is an email service. Each morning we read new US federal contract notices published on SAM.gov, select the ones that match the trades, states and eligibility you give us, and email them to you. Where we can, we add information from USAspending.gov about the likely current or previous contract for the same work. Your alerts also carry reminders and updates for bids we sent you and, on Mondays, federal contracts in your states that end in 3 to 6 months. We email you only on days when there is something new for you, and on a Monday after a week with nothing new, one short note saying so. We also publish free public pages listing open federal cleaning notices by state. We are not a government service, a bidding agent, a law firm or a consultant.
