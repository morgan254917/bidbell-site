Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

Your name, business name, business email, the trades and states you choose, and your eligibility details (for example small business or veteran-owned). Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy; we never see or store your card details.

Why

Only to send you matching bid alerts, run your subscription and answer your emails.

Business outreach

We may email businesses once or a few times about BidBell using business contact details they publish on their own websites or in public contract records. Every such email says how to opt out, and we stop immediately when asked.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services that run BidBell: Google Workspace (email), GitHub (running the alert program) and Lemon Squeezy (payments).

Your choices

Email hello@getbidbell.com to see, correct or delete your data, or reply "no" to any email to stop receiving them.
