Email Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Who we email

We send a small number of emails to businesses that may need BidBell: commercial cleaning companies (janitorial, carpet and window cleaning), including companies that have won federal contracts, at a business email address published on their own website, their own business pages (such as Facebook or Google) or in public US government records. We never buy email lists or use data brokers, and we never guess addresses.

What every email contains

  • Our real name and business name, and an honest subject line.
  • A plain statement that it is a sales email about a paid service, with the price.
  • Our mailing address: 1670 Broadway, Denver, CO 80202, United States.
  • A clear way to stop: reply “no” and we will not email you again.

We send each company one email, never a follow-up.

Opting out

Reply “no” or “unsubscribe”, or email hello@getbidbell.com. We act on it promptly, and always within 10 business days, as the US CAN-SPAM Act requires. Your address goes on our do-not-contact list so it is never emailed again, and we do not email anyone else at your company either.

No tracking

Our emails contain no tracking pixels or hidden images.

Subscriber alerts

Alert emails go only to people who asked for them. To change or stop them, reply to any alert.

Report a problem

If you think we emailed you in error, tell us at hello@getbidbell.com.
