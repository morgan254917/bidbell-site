Refund Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

New customers can try BidBell free for 14 days, with no card needed, so you can judge the alerts before you pay.

30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with BidBell, we will refund any payment in full, on the monthly or the yearly plan, if you ask within 30 days of that payment.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@getbidbell.com, or reply to any BidBell email, within 30 days of the payment. You can also call us at (913) 252-0636. You can also ask Paddle directly, through the help link in your receipt or at paddle.net.

How refunds are paid

Our payments are handled by Paddle.com, our merchant of record, so Paddle issues your refund to your original payment method. How long it takes to show depends on your bank or card provider.

Cancelling

Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel anytime: email hello@getbidbell.com, reply to any BidBell email, or use the “Manage subscription” link in your receipt from Paddle. After you cancel, you are not charged again, and your alerts continue to the end of the period you have paid for.
